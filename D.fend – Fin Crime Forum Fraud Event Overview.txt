D.fend – Fin Crime Forum Fraud Landscape Survey / Fraud Event Overview
Project Overview

Deloitte is conducting a market survey to support the upcoming D.fend – Fin Crime Forum, a fraud-focused event bringing together senior leaders across banking and insurance to discuss emerging fraud risks, industry trends, operational challenges, and leading practices.

The survey is intended to generate quantitative market insights that will:

Inform event agenda development and discussion topics.
Identify the issues most relevant to fraud leaders today.
Benchmark current industry practices and future priorities.
Provide data-driven insights that can be incorporated into presentations, breakout sessions, and executive discussions.
Potentially support future Deloitte thought leadership and points of view (POVs).

Target Audience

Key Research Objectives

The survey seeks to understand 5 overarching topics:

1. Deepfake and Emerging Fraud Risks
Where organizations are experiencing the greatest deepfake-related vulnerabilities.
Key challenges preventing effective deepfake detection and mitigation.
2. Fraud Information Sharing
Barriers preventing effective information sharing.
Government and industry actions that could improve collaboration.
Industry appetite for greater data sharing and consortium models.
3. Third-Party Fraud Risk
Which third parties currently create the greatest fraud exposure.
How organizations plan to reduce third-party fraud risk in the future.
4. Fraud Operating Model Transformation
How organizations are scaling fraud operations today.
The role of automation, AI, outsourcing, and process redesign.
Future operational transformation priorities.
5. Regulatory and Market Change
How regulatory requirements are influencing fraud investment.
Which upcoming changes are expected to most significantly impact fraud programs.


The survey should provide a data-driven view of how leading financial institutions and insurers are responding to a rapidly evolving fraud landscape and help shape meaningful discussions at the D.fend – Fin Crime Forum around the future of fraud risk management.
